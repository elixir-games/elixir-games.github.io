What to watch for

Good positions in Mills look stable from far away and dangerous up close. The best turns often threaten two futures at once.

Protect the points that connect more than one line.
Do not give away easy resets that let a rival rebuild pressure.
Use removals to break mobility, not just to lower stone count.
Keep at least one path open for the move after your best move.
