Our story

About

Make small games that delight and scale — craft over scope, performance over bloat.

Founded in 2020 by a team of tooling and gameplay engineers, Elixir Games builds compact, high-performance gaming experiences and open-source developer prototypes. We believe in keeping games performant, accessible, and fun.

Core values

01

Player-First Design

We focus on immediate accessibility and deep, satisfying mechanics.

02

Measurable Performance

Rock-solid frame rates and instant loads, optimized for any device.

03

Open Tooling

We regularly release our underlying engines and libraries back to the community.

04

Respectful Experiences

Zero tracking bloat, zero attention-hogging loops — just great gameplay.
