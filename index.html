Our Story

Founded in 2020 by a team of tooling and gameplay engineers, Elixir Games builds compact, high-performance gaming experiences and open-source developer prototypes. We believe in keeping games performant, accessible, and fun.

Core Values

  • Player-First Design: We focus on immediate accessibility and deep, satisfying mechanics.
  • Measurable Performance: Rock-solid frame rates and instant loads, optimized for any device.
  • Open Tooling: We regularly release our underlying engines and libraries back to the community.
  • Respectful Experiences: Zero tracking bloat, zero attention-hogging loops — just great gameplay.
